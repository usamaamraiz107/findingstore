About FindingStore

FindingStore is a free price-comparison tool built around a simple idea: you should be able to find the cheapest place to buy something even when you don't know what it's called. Upload a photo of a product, or paste an image link, and FindingStore shows where similar items are sold, with the lowest prices first.

What it does

  • Identifies the product from your photo using visual search, and builds a plain-English description from the matches.
  • Lists local stores for your country (detected automatically, and you can change it), sorted by price.
  • Lists international stores separately, so you can see whether importing is worth it.
  • Separates exact and similar matches, so look-alike items don't get mixed up with the one you want.

Where the data comes from

Results come from Google search and Google Lens data, accessed through the SerpAPI service. Prices, titles and images belong to the stores that list them. We don't sell anything ourselves and we don't hold stock. Prices taken from ordinary search results, rather than from structured shopping data, are labelled "from search results, verify on store".

Our principles

  • No fake prices. If the service is running without live data, a clear demo banner says so.
  • Honest ranking. Results are ordered by price and by how closely they match your photo, not by payment from stores.
  • Minimal data. We don't require an account, and uploaded photos are deleted automatically by the temporary host. Read our Privacy Policy.
